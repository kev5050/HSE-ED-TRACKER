      Not sure it's an emergency?

      Phone your GP or out-of-hours GP. For a minor injury, like a sprain, a small cut or a suspected simple fracture, an Injury Unit is often faster. Each one has its own age limit and hours. If the injury looks serious, go to an emergency department or call 112 or 999.

      "Busier than usual" compares the people waiting for a bed at 8am with that hospital's recent days. It is not a waiting time.
